# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

People, families, public employees, and small or medium businesses in Mozambique who need short-term credit for purchases, urgent expenses, trade, guarantees, protection, or business cash flow

## Product Purpose

Present Nathium's credit products clearly, help visitors estimate repayments, and turn financial intent into a qualified conversation with the lending team

## Brand Personality

Confident, direct, and premium, with a strong money-first voice that remains responsible and transparent

## Anti-references

Avoid loud casino-like financial visuals, frantic motion, speculative wealth promises, generic fintech dashboards, excessive animation, and copy that hides costs or implies guaranteed approval

## Design Principles

- Make money and its practical use the clearest idea on every page
- Build trust by showing costs, terms, and next steps plainly
- Use calm, premium pacing instead of aggressive urgency
- Keep interactions direct and useful, especially simulation and contact flows
- Let imagery and restrained motion communicate momentum without distraction

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and interaction standards, support keyboard navigation, preserve readable Portuguese copy, and fully respect reduced-motion preferences
